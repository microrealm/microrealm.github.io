中文

微界（英文名 MicroRealm）是一款微距景深堆叠相机 App，由个人开发者 qiong wu 在 App Store 上提供。我们非常重视你的隐私。简单说：微界不收集、不上传任何个人数据。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有任何第三方 SDK。

1. 相机

微界使用相机拍摄微距对焦包围照片。拍到的照片只保存在你手机上微界自己的存储空间里，堆叠合成也完全在你的手机上完成，不会发送到任何服务器。

2. 相册

只有在你主动点「保存到相册」时，微界才会把合成好的照片写入你的系统相册。微界只申请「添加照片」权限，不会读取你相册里的其他照片。

3. 本地网络（浏览器遥控）

只有在你主动开启「浏览器遥控」时，微界才会在手机上开启一个仅限局域网访问的小服务，让同一 Wi-Fi 或个人热点下的平板、手机用浏览器遥控拍摄。实时画面和遥控指令只在这两台设备之间直接传输，不经过任何服务器，也不连接互联网；连接需要每次随机生成的口令。关闭遥控或离开微界后服务即停止。

4. 运动传感器

微界读取陀螺仪数据，用来判断手机是否已经静止（静止后才开拍，避免晃动）。这些数据只在拍摄时实时使用，不保存、不上传。

5. 本机存储的设置

镜头距离标定、自动学习到的镜头光学参数等设置保存在你的手机上，仅供微界自己使用。

6. 删除你的数据

在微界的图库里删除项目即可删除对应照片；卸载微界会删除它保存的全部照片和设置。我们不持有你的任何数据，所以没有需要在服务器上删除的内容。

7. TestFlight 测试版

如果你通过苹果的 TestFlight 参加微界的测试，苹果会按照其 TestFlight 隐私说明 收集崩溃日志、使用信息以及你主动提交的反馈和截图，并提供给开发者，仅用于改进微界。

8. 儿童隐私

微界不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

9. 政策变更

如果微界今后的功能涉及新的数据处理方式，我们会先更新本页并修改生效日期。

10. 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 microrealm.app@outlook.com。

English

MicroRealm (微界) is a macro focus-stacking camera app provided on the App Store by the individual developer qiong wu. In short: MicroRealm does not collect or upload any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

1. Camera

MicroRealm uses the camera to shoot macro focus brackets. Photos are stored only in the app's own storage on your device, and focus stacking runs entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

2. Photo Library

MicroRealm writes a stacked photo to your photo library only when you tap "Save to Photos". It requests add-only access and never reads the other photos in your library.

3. Local Network (Browser Remote)

Only when you turn on Browser Remote does MicroRealm start a small local-network-only service, so that a tablet or phone on the same Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot can control shooting from a web browser. The live view and control commands travel directly between those two devices — never through any server and never over the internet — and each connection requires a randomly generated passcode. The service stops when you turn the remote off or leave the app.

4. Motion Sensors

MicroRealm reads the gyroscope to tell when your phone is still, so it starts shooting only after vibrations settle. This data is used in real time while shooting and is never stored or uploaded.

5. Settings Stored on Your Device

Settings such as lens distance calibration and learned lens optics are stored on your device for MicroRealm's own use only.

6. Deleting Your Data

Deleting a project in MicroRealm's gallery deletes its photos; deleting the app removes all of its photos and settings. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to delete on any server.

7. TestFlight Beta

If you test MicroRealm through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs, usage information, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to submit, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice, and shares them with the developer solely to improve MicroRealm.

8. Children's Privacy

MicroRealm does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If a future feature handles data differently, we will update this page and its effective date first.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email microrealm.app@outlook.com.
